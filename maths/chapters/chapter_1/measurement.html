1. Measuring with Hands

Before rulers and measuring tapes, people measured objects using their hands. Now we are going to learn how we can measure objects with our hands!

Hand Measurement Meaning Example
👆 Finger Width Width of one finger We can use our finger to measure the width of crayons, pencils, sketch pens, etc.
✋ Palm Width of the hand We can use our palm to measure our books, notebooks, pencil boxes, etc.
🖐️ Hand Span Distance from thumb to little finger when stretched. We can measure the width of our notebook, table, bench, and desk with our hand span.
Activity:

Measure these objects using your hand span:
  • 📚 Your book
  • 🪑 Your chair
  • 🖥️ Your computer table
  • 📝 Your notebook
  • 💵 10 rupees note
Ask your friend to measure the same objects. Did you both get the same answer?

2. Measurement with a Scale (Ruler)

Now that we have learned measurement with hands, let's learn how to measure accurately using a scale (ruler).

  • Place the object along the ruler.
  • Start measuring from the 0 mark.
  • Read the number at the other end of the object.
  • The length is written in centimetres (cm) or millimetres (mm).
Examples:

✏️ Pencil = 15 cm
📖 Book = 20 cm
📱 Mobile Phone = 14 cm
