Mount Everest

Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world. It is part of the Himalayan Mountain Range and lies on the border of Nepal and China.

Key Facts

  • 📍 Located in Asia
  • 📏 Height: 8,849 metres
  • 🏔️ Highest mountain on Earth
  • 🥾 Climbers from around the world visit every year
⭐ Fun Fact: Mount Everest is still growing a little taller every year.

K2

K2 is the second-highest mountain in the world. It is located in the Karakoram Range between Pakistan and China.

Key Facts

  • 📏 Height: 8,611 metres
  • 🌍 Second-highest mountain
  • 🧗 One of the hardest mountains to climb
  • ❄️ Covered with snow all year
⭐ Fun Fact: K2 is often called the "Savage Mountain" because of its difficult climbing conditions.

Kangchenjunga

Kangchenjunga is the third-highest mountain in the world. It lies on the border between India and Nepal.

Key Facts

  • 📏 Height: 8,586 metres
  • 🇮🇳 Located between India and Nepal
  • 🏔️ Part of the Himalayas
  • 🌨️ Covered with glaciers and snow
⭐ Fun Fact: Kangchenjunga means "The Five Treasures of Snow."

Mount Kilimanjaro

Mount Kilimanjaro is the highest mountain in Africa. It is a dormant volcano located in Tanzania.

Key Facts

  • 🌍 Located in Africa
  • 📏 Height: 5,895 metres
  • 🌋 Dormant volcano
  • 🥾 Popular with hikers
⭐ Fun Fact: You can see snow at the top even though it is near the Equator.

Mount Fuji

Mount Fuji is Japan's highest mountain. It is a beautiful volcanic mountain and an important cultural symbol of Japan.

Key Facts

  • 🇯🇵 Located in Japan
  • 📏 Height: 3,776 metres
  • 🌋 Active volcano
  • 📸 Famous for its snowy peak
⭐ Fun Fact: Mount Fuji is one of the most photographed mountains in the world.
