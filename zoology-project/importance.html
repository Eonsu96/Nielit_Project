Ecological Importance

Animals play an important role in maintaining ecological balance. They help in pollination, seed dispersal, and maintaining food chains and food webs in ecosystems.

  • Maintain balance in ecosystems
  • Help in nutrient cycling
  • Control population of other organisms

Economic Importance

Animals are a major source of income and resources for humans. They provide food, clothing, and materials used in various industries.

  • Milk, meat, eggs, and honey
  • Wool, leather, and silk
  • Employment in agriculture and dairy industries

Scientific and Educational Importance

Animals are widely used in scientific research and education. Studying animals helps us understand biological processes and develop new medical treatments.

  • Used in laboratory research
  • Help in medical advancements
  • Important for education and awareness
