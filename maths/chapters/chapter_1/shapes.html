1. Shapes and Their Names

Shapes are all around us! We see them in books, toys, buildings, and nature. Let's learn some common shapes.

Shape Name Example
⚪ Circle Clock, plate, bangle, ring
🟦 Square Chessboard, Window
▭ Rectangle Door, Book
🔺 Triangle Pizza Slice, Traffic Sign
⭐ Star Star in the Sky
❤️ Heart Greeting Card
⬟ Pentagon Home Plate (Baseball)
⬡ Hexagon Honeycomb
⬢ Octagon Stop Sign
💎 Diamond (Rhombus) Kite
Fun Activity:
  • Find one circular object in your classroom.
  • Find one square object.
  • Find one rectangular object.
  • Can you find a triangle?
