What is Animal Diversity?

Animal diversity refers to the wide variety of animals found on Earth. Animals differ in their body structure, habitat, mode of nutrition, and way of life. This diversity allows animals to survive in different environmental conditions.

Invertebrates

Invertebrates are animals that do not have a backbone. They form the largest group of animals and are found in almost all habitats.

  • Porifera - Sponges
  • Coelenterata - Jellyfish
  • Annelida - Earthworms
  • Arthropoda - Insects
  • Mollusca - Snails

Vertebrates

Vertebrates are animals that possess a backbone. They are more complex in structure and have a well-developed nervous system.

  • Pisces - Fishes
  • Amphibia - Frogs
  • Reptilia - Snakes
  • Aves - Birds
  • Mammalia - Humans
