What is Animal Classification?

Animal Classification is the scientific method of arranging animals into groups based on their similarities and differences. It helps scientists to study, identify, and understand the relationships between different animal species.

Levels of Classification

Animals are classified into different levels. These levels form a hierarchy from broader groups to more specific groups.

  • Kingdom:The largest group which includes all animals
  • Phylum:Groups animals based on body structure
  • Class:Divides phylum into smaller groups
  • Order:Further classification within a class
  • Family:Groups closely related animals
  • Genus:A group of similar species
  • Species:The most specific level of classification
