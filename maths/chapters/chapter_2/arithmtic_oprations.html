1. Addition

  • Addition means finding the total.
  • The symbol for addition is +.
  • The answer of an addition is called the sum.

Examples

$$2 + 3 = 5$$

$$5 + 4 = 9$$

$$10 + 6 = 16$$

$$15 + 8 = 23$$

$$25 + 12 = 37$$

2. Subtraction

  • Subtraction means taking away.
  • The symbol for subtraction is −.
  • The answer of a subtraction is called the difference.

Examples

$$8 - 3 = 5$$

$$15 - 7 = 8$$

$$20 - 9 = 11$$

$$35 - 12 = 23$$

$$50 - 25 = 25$$

3. Multiples & Multiplication

Multiples are obtained by multiplying a number by 1, 2, 3, 4, and so on.

Example: Multiples of 1

$$1 \times 1 = 1$$

$$1 \times 2 = 2$$

$$1 \times 3 = 3$$

$$1 \times 4 = 4$$

$$1 \times 5 = 5$$

4. Division

Division means sharing or separating objects into equal groups. The symbol used for division is (÷).

Division means sharing objects into equal groups. The answer of a division is called the quotient.
  • Division means sharing equally.
  • The symbol for division is ÷.
  • The answer of a division is called the quotient.
