Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet in our Solar System.

Key Facts

  • ☀️ Closest planet to the Sun
  • 🪨 Smallest planet
  • 🌡️ Very hot during the day and very cold at night
  • 🌙 Has no moons
⭐ Fun Fact: A year on Mercury lasts only 88 Earth days.

Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is often called Earth's twin because it is almost the same size.

Key Facts

  • 🌍 Similar in size to Earth
  • 🔥 Hottest planet in the Solar System
  • ☁️ Covered with thick clouds
  • 🌙 Has no moons
⭐ Fun Fact: Venus rotates in the opposite direction from most planets.

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet that supports life.

Key Facts

  • 💧 About 71% covered by water
  • 🌱 Home to millions of living things
  • 🌙 Has one natural moon
  • 🌍 Our home planet
⭐ Fun Fact: Earth is often called the Blue Planet.

Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is known as the Red Planet.

Key Facts

  • 🔴 Known as the Red Planet
  • 🛰️ Has two small moons
  • 🚀 Many space missions have explored Mars
  • 🗻 Home to Olympus Mons, the largest volcano
⭐ Fun Fact: A day on Mars is almost the same length as a day on Earth.

Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System and is made mostly of gases.

Key Facts

  • 🪐 Largest planet
  • 🌪️ Famous for the Great Red Spot
  • 🌙 Has more than 90 known moons
  • 💨 Gas giant
⭐ Fun Fact: More than 1,300 Earths could fit inside Jupiter.

Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and is famous for its beautiful rings.

Key Facts

  • 💍 Spectacular ring system
  • 💨 Gas giant
  • 🌙 Has more than 140 known moons
  • 🪐 Second-largest planet
⭐ Fun Fact: Saturn could float on water because it is less dense than water.

Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and is known for spinning on its side.

Key Facts

  • 🧊 Ice giant
  • 🔄 Rotates on its side
  • 💍 Has faint rings
  • 🌙 Has 28 known moons
⭐ Fun Fact: Uranus appears blue because of methane gas in its atmosphere.

Neptune

Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun. It is a cold, windy ice giant.

Key Facts

  • 🌬️ Strongest winds in the Solar System
  • 🧊 Ice giant
  • 🌙 Has 16 known moons
  • 🔵 Deep blue in colour
⭐ Fun Fact: One year on Neptune lasts about 165 Earth years.
